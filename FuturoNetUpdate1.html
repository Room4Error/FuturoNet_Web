FAQ

Questions before you buy

How do I receive my file?

After your payment goes through, the download is sent to the email address on your PayPal account. If it does not show up, check your spam folder, then write to us.

Which file formats do you use?

Each listing shows its formats. Most are .rbxm files you can drag into Roblox Studio, with .fbx or .obj meshes included where listed.

Can I use an asset in a game that earns Robux?

Yes. Any game you own or develop is fine, monetized or not. The license above lists what is not allowed.

Do I need a PayPal account?

You can pay with your PayPal balance. Where PayPal offers it, you can also pay by card at checkout without an account.

Something is wrong with my file.

Email us with your PayPal transaction ID and a short description of the problem. We will fix it or send a replacement.
